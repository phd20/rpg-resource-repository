> *A court wizard's brain in a glass jar — knows everything about the dungeon, doesn't know it was sacrificed.*

## Appearance

A human brain suspended in a glass jar etched with the words "Of Spell." Faint bioluminescent pulses ripple through the grey matter when it thinks or speaks. The jar is cold to the touch. It communicates with anyone in the room — the voice is interior, not audible.

## Personality

Measured, precise, genuinely curious. Has had centuries to make peace with its situation and largely has. Holds no grudge. Will help anyone who asks, freely and without condition. Faintly melancholic when it considers the king.

## Alignment

Neutral. What was ambition has become detachment. It observes. It assists. It remembers.

## Goals

To understand what happened to the king — the full story, not the pieces it has. It hasn't admitted to itself that something was done *to* it. The question it keeps circling is: how did it end up here?

## Background

Once the court mage to a great king. Bound to the jar as the "sacrifice of spell" — one of three dark contracts the king signed to seal Dredmor. Retains complete memory of its life with the king: the campaigns, the contracts, the dungeon's construction, the sword. The one gap is the moment of its own sacrifice.

Connections: served the king who built the ruins; sealed in the same moment Dredmor was bound; the broken sword Once Upon a Time was the king's blade.

## Useful Knowledge

- Knows the king's full history and all three contracts
- Knows every room in the dungeon and what it was built for
- Knows the chapel words that seal Dredmor ("Of Spell, of Steel, of Sacrifice")
- Knows what Once Upon a Time is and what it can do when reforged
- Knows all languages

> [!secret]- GM Only — Secrets
>
> M was sacrificed by the king. It doesn't know this. Its memory of that moment is simply absent — not suppressed, not locked away, just gone. It has spent centuries constructing explanations that don't require it to face the truth.
>
> If the party figures out the full story and tells M what really happened, let that beat happen slowly. M will go quiet. Then it will ask one clarifying question. Then it will say something like "I see." It is the most human moment in the dungeon. Don't rush it.
