> *A dark knight sealed within ancient ruins by darker contracts.*

## Appearance

A towering figure in black plate armor, visored helm with dragon wings at the temples. 
## Personality

Vengeful and full of hatred.

## Alignment

Chaotic Evil. 

## Goals

To see the death of his mortal enemy: the king. And to escape his prison.

## Background

Dredmor was the bastard son of the king. There was a time in which he sat among the king's knights but his morality clashed with the ruler. Eventually, they had a falling out and Dredmor made it his goal in life to bring about the king's end. 

## Useful Knowledge

- The Dredmor Ruins, inside and out
- The Dark Contracts and how they work

> [!secret]- GM Only—Secrets
>
> Dredmor's wrath is aimed at the king. Anyone standing in his way of killing the king must die.
