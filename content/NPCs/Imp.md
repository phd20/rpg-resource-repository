> *A minor devil brokering soul contracts on behalf of an archdevil — grinning, transactional, and not entirely here by coincidence.*

## Appearance

Small and wiry, red-skinned, with vestigial leathery wings and a barbed stinger tail. Always grinning. Smells faintly of sulfur. Moves with the skittering energy of something that has never had to sit still.

## Personality

Gleefully transactional. Everything is an offer. It frames every deal as generosity and every demand as reasonable. It enjoys the negotiation more than the outcome. Will not fight unless cornered — violence is for people who ran out of angles.

## Alignment

Chaotic. Serves its archdevil patron's standing directive, but follows its own amusements until called upon. The two don't always point the same direction.

## Goals

To broker a soul contract — ideally with someone who wants something badly enough to sign. Has been digging in Room 7 for the broken sword; the blade is useful leverage in negotiations. Secondary goal: find out if Dredmor can be made useful to the archdevil.

## Background

A minor devil operating under standing orders to collect soul contracts on behalf of an archdevil. Found its way into the ruins through the same infernal channels the king trafficked with when he signed the dark contracts. The imp has no formal role here — it simply showed up and made itself at home.

Connections: operates under an unnamed archdevil; traffics in the same dark-contract economy the king used; has an interest in Dredmor that goes beyond idle curiosity.

## Useful Knowledge

- Knows the terms of the dark contracts the king signed
- Can arrange patronage from the archdevil — boons, power, protection — for a soul
- Knows what Once Upon a Time is and that it's worth something
- Knows Dredmor is bound and roughly why

> [!secret]- GM Only — Secrets
>
> The archdevil the imp serves has a specific interest in Dredmor — whether as an asset, a debtor, or a problem to manage is left to the GM. The imp's presence in these ruins is not random.
>
> The imp will sign a contract with a player character if they're willing. The contract is real — the archdevil will collect. The imp won't explain the fine print unless directly asked, and even then will answer technically but not helpfully.
