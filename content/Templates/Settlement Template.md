## Overview

*Describe the settlement. What is its function? How is it familiar? What's the fantastic element?*

## Atmosphere

*What is the energy of the settlement? What are the sights, smells, and sounds? What stands out to someone first approaching the city? What story does the architecture tell?*

## Size

*While an exact population isn't necessary, a rough population size helps inform the experience of the settlement. Examples of types include metropolis, large city, small city, town, village, hamlet, etc.*

## Inhabitants

*What species inhabit the settlement? What are the demographics the party can expect to encounter? These definitions help inspire interesting encounter design.*

## Government

*What form of government rules this settlement? Who are the key players in making decisions? This can be a one word answer like "meritocracy, council, patriarchy, democracy, etc" or it can include a few details about the rulers and how they rule.*

## Defenses

*How does this settlement defend itself? Think in terms of natural defenses (waterways, mountains, etc), built defenses (towers, walls, motes, etc), and people defenses (city guard, local militia, wizards, etc).*

## Commerce

*What does the industry and trade look like for this settlement? What goods are produced and sold here? What goods are brought in from elsewhere?*

## History

*A brief history of this place.*

## Organizations

*What organizations hold power within the settlement? What factions are ready to provide quests? What religions are ready for worship? What guilds seek new members? Starting with at least **three** organizations that can provide conflict, quests, or information is perfect.*
