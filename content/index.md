The RPG Resource Repository is a public, version-controlled library of GM content for tabletop RPGs. It's free forever, under Creative Commons. Stop by for inspiration, take artifacts for your games, or download the whole thing as an Obsidian vault.

## Design Philosophy

The framework behind every piece of content lives in **[[PRINCIPLES]]**. The core model: **Backend** (what is true) → **Runtime** (what you run live). Backend artifacts are standalone sources of truth that slot into any campaign. Runtime documents orchestrate them at the table.
