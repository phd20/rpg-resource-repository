> *A cruel mace of obsidian and steel.*

## Description

A heavy, wicked mace with a head of black obsidian, polished to a mirror finish. 

## Properties

```
MALICE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
+1 mace.

Profane. You suffer -2 to reaction rolls for all clergy.
```

## History

This is a weapon forged in some dark corner of the world. Priests have long known its name and trembled. 

> [!secret]- GM Only
>
> The effects of the mace are immediate so long as it is carried. It does not need to be in view for clergy to sense something wrong and react poorly.
