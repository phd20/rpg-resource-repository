> *A demented dagger with a small skull pommel.*

## Description

This dagger has an unsettlingly thin blade and a skull pommel that either whispers or filters the breeze in a strange way. 

## Properties

```
TORMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
+1 dagger.

Blinded to Gold. You can no longer see anything made of gold.
```

## History

Torment would often find its way into noble households, striking terror into the wealthy and greedy alike. Few recognized the dagger was the source of their madness.

> [!secret]- GM Only
> The character wielding this weapon is unlikely to understand the effect initially. At first, it might manifest as seeing party members speaking nonsense. Lean in to the idea that *they* are seeing things rather than this character is *not* seeing them.
