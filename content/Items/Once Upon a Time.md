> *A mythical longsword that once belonged to a great king.*

## Description

Shards of a longsword with a story lost to time. Largely unremarkable as a blade, the exquisite craftsmanship is undeniable. Despite being broken, this sword shows little to no signs of age or use. 
## Properties

```
ONCE UPON A TIME
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
+2 longsword.

Exploding Damage. The damage dice explode. If you roll maximum
damage on a die, roll again and add it to the total. No cap.
```

## History

The Once Upon a Time blade was long ago pulled from a stone. It made a king, forged a kingdom, and ultimately paid a price.

> [!secret]- Secrets
>
> If it isn't already clear, this sword is Excalibur. If this blade is reforged and brought back to the stone it was pulled from, a great boon awaits.
