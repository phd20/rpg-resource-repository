> Jastir is a **dark and doom-filled** (deals at night, acts with doom and gloom) **purveyor of the prohibited** (acts as a fence, stocks illegal goods). 

## Appearance

Jastir wears a heavy coat of leather and a dark cloak. He hides a scarred and troubled face.

## Personality

Jastir is constantly looking around as if nervous or expecting something bad to happen. He's a salesman and merchant at heart but an awkward interaction for most.

## Alignment

Chaotic neutral.

## Goals

Jastir simply wants to survive.

## Background

Jastir was a promising young merchant. But he caught the temptation of illicit goods. Before he knew it, he was trapped in a world he regretted but wouldn't dare escape. 

## Useful Knowledge

- Where to find nearly anything illegal.

> [!secret]- GM Only—Secrets
>
> Jastir sells goods on behalf of the warlord Akuran. He fears for his life.
