> *A wizard's brain, now in a jar.*

## Appearance

A grey human brain floats in a jar of greenish-blue liquid. Etched haphazardly into the glass of the jar are the words "Of Spell."
## Personality

The wizard's brain is much older than it seems—centuries at least. It has fleeting memories of serving a king and specific quests to conquer and protect a kingdom. But the details are sparse and it struggles to piece them together. It comes across as confused and lost. 

## Alignment

Chaotic Neutral.

## Background

This was the court mage to one of the greatest kings in history. A druid at heart, this mage helped the king conquer lands unknown and battle foul evils. But at some point, the king sacrificed the mage.

## Useful Knowledge

> M doesn't know it was sacrificed. It hasn't figured out its current state. If it pieces that together, it might struggle to accept it at first. But once it does, it might cause a powerful and dangerous arcane energy storm.
