> A rambunctious storyteller often found at the Inn of Good Faith. 

## Appearance

He's balding with long hair and a long white beard that comes to a point. 

## Personality

**Dramatic aplomb** (master storyteller, brilliant showmanship). **Slow and gentle** (slow outside his stories, gentle and friendly).

## Alignment

*Their alignment and how it manifests.

## Goals

To entertain and tell great stories.

## Background

Old Mott is a traveling storyteller and has been for decades. He used to explore a lot of the world but in his older years, he frequents the same places where he knows he has an audience.

## Useful Knowledge

- Old Mott knows a great deal about stories across different cultures

> He’s abandoned many wives in many cities.
