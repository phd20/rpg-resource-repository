> *The proud and loud dwarf proprietor of* Isathil's Good Trades.

## Appearance

This dwarf's immaculate hair and beard flow so cleanly into each other that he may as well have a lion's mane. His bushy mustache and eyebrows dominate his face while soft eyes happily meet anyone he comes across.

## Personality

**Proud and loud** (lives for his trade, booming voice).

## Alignment

Lawful good.

## Goals

To acquire and sell the best goods around.

## Background

Isathil was raised on the road—the son of a traveling merchant. While he didn't grow up amongst dwarven culture, his mother did a fine job teaching him of his ancestry and heritage. He was quick to the family business and eventually settled in Destiny's Point with a stall.

## Useful Knowledge

-  **Well-connected** (contacts in every city, on every road).
