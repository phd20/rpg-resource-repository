> The owner of Fallen Star Alchemy is an incredible alchemist...and an even more incredible asshole.

## Appearance

He’s known for a great white beard that reaches his knees. **Old and brittle** (bones pop with each step, hunched over).

## Personality

**Crass and unforgiving** (insults your intelligence, short on manners). 

## Alignment

Lawful neutral.

## Goals

Morlyn has mastered alchemy and strives to discover new and exciting formulations.

## Background

Morlyn comes from a far away land but opened Fallen Star Alchemy in Destiny's Point because of the proximity to rare alchemical reserves.

## Useful Knowledge

- The alchemical ingredients of the area
- Alchemical formulae
