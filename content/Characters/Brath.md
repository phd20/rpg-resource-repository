> *A town guard captain with the presence and personality of a noble knight.*

## Appearance

**Clean and lean** (always clean-shaven, fit physique).

## Personality

**Pleasing polyglot** (chivalrous, speaks Common, Dwarvish, Elvish, Giant, Draconic). He acts and presents himself in a manner that far exceeds his actual station.

## Alignment

Lawful good. Brath leans more towards the law but if pressed on his morals, he'd be willing to sacrifice the law for the greater good. 

## Goals

Brath wants to protect his hometown of Destiny's Point at any cost. 

## Background

Brath grew up a brash young boy in Destiny's Point. He played in the nearby woods until a friend was lost to some horrific monster. He swore to defend everyone at any cost. He became the leader of the town guard and quite the intellect, as well.

## Useful Knowledge

- A handful of uncommon languages: Dwarvish, Elvish, Giant, and Draconic
- The nearby region around his town and the threats that are present

> [!secret]- GM Only—Secrets
>
> He believes that some townsfolk are members of a secret cult.
