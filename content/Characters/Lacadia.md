> *An elderly innkeeper who will beat you at arm wrestling before serving you dinner.*

## Appearance

**Apron and ladle** (always has a red apron, carries a ladle). **Slow but strong** (moves slowly due to age, wins arm wrestling contests).

## Personality

Lacadia speaks slowly but intently. 

## Alignment

Chaotic good.

## Goals

Lacadia wants to build a welcoming community in her town, starting with her inn.

## Background

Lacadia grew up as part of a traveling caravan. Daughter to a theater performer and a chef, she was an artisan at an early age. Her family settled in Destiny's Point and founded the Inn of Good Faith. She's worked there ever since.
## Useful Knowledge

- History of her town, even from events long before her arrival.

> [!secret]- GM Only—Secrets
>
> Lacadia had a falling out with her family due to a hag’s curse.
