> The wise and mysterious Elder of Destiny's Point.

## Appearance

A groomed white mustache sits atop a long and smooth beard. His sharp lines and wrinkled skin cast shadows across his face in the right light. His eyes are deep with thought.

## Personality

**Disciplined and wise** (blunt with friends, kind to strangers). **Slow and scholarly** (heavy breathing, always in his library).

## Alignment

Chaotic Good.

## Goals

Nicodaemus is intent on gathering and recording the hidden knowledge of the region. He believes the town will not survive without it.

## Background

Nicodaemus grew up in Destiny's Point. He was your typical wild child, running through the woods and playing in the streams. As a young adult, he left the town and traveled with the Quildan (traveling groups of wanderers, bards and craftsmen). He returned changed but quickly found himself leading the town.
