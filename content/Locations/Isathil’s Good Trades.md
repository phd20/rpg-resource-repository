> *"Welcome! Have a seat. Have a drink! I even get the good stuff from far in the north. Hag’s Whiskey or a Dragontooth Stout, on the house while you shop around!"*

Isathil Thunderstone is famous for having more goods than any other shop in town, often more than can be found in the Aureum. He hails from Alheim but made his home in Destiny’s Point.

![[Isathil's Good Trades _title no logo_.webp]]

### Equipment

At Isathil’s Good Trades, you can find any standard adventuring gear. He carries a limited supply of weapons along with armor and shields but can always order more. He also stocks a vast assortment of equipment packs and tools. 

His shop is expensive (10% above standard) but Isathil is a _great_ contact for any adventuring party. He’s well-connected throughout Destiny’s Point and with travelers beyond. All of his equipment is guaranteed.

### Treasure

Isathil is always willing to appraise and make offers for treasure you might find. He’s considered fair in his appraisals. The dwarf is particularly interested in precious gems, art objects, and interesting trinkets.

**Magic Items.** Isathil is connected enough that he occasionally has a magic item or two in stock. There’s a 5% chance that he has a rare magic item and a 15% chance that he has an uncommon magic item.

### Quests

#### Fraud

A week ago, Isathil came across an orc down on his luck. Out of pity, he sold the orc parchment and an ink pen to write a letter to his family asking for help. Thanks to a special watermark, Isathil discovered the parchment was used as a forged bill of sale for goods from the local blacksmith. 

Find the fraudster and turn him in to the local guards or “deal with him” and Isathil will pay 250 gp. He’ll even throw in a few trinkets from the shop.

#### Shimmering Smile

A traveler spoke of a troll skull with gems for teeth at a shrine up near Mount Ebari. But a large and hostile creature guards it.

Investigate the shrine to either validate or disprove the story and Isathil will pay 100 gp. Bring him the skull in question _with_ the gems and he’ll pay 10 times that amount.
