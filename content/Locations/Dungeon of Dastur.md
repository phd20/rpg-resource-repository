## Overview

The Dungeon of Dastur is the meeting place of the Cult of Dastur—an evil but secretive group in Destiny's Point.

## Conditions

```
DARKNESS: Areas with light sources have dim light. All other areas are dark.
DOORS: Doors are locked, unless otherwise noted.
REACTIONS: Cult members are hostile and will attempt to capture or kill.
```
## Atmosphere

This dungeon has trapped the horrible memories of its past within the very walls. Some bleed, others release faint screams. It smells of mold and detritus.

> [!secret]- GM Only—Secrets & Change
>
> Cult members only occupy this space during special times of year that are sacred to their beliefs. They offer living sacrifices at the statue.
