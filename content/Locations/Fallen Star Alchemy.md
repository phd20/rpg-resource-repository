The bubbling of brews and alchemical aromas fill the air of Fallen Star Alchemy, one of the oldest businesses in the region.

### Goods

The following items are available for purchase at Fallen Star Alchemy. Each day, roll 1d10 to determine which item is half price.

| D10 | Good                        | Price (gp) |
| --- | --------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1   | Oil of Slipperiness         | 480        |
| 2   | Potion of Animal Friendship | 200        |
| 3   | Potion of Climbing          | 180        |
| 4   | Potion of Heroism           | 180        |
| 5   | Potion of Poison            | 100        |
| 6   | Potion of Healing           | 50         |
| 7   | Potion of Speed             | 400        |
| 8   | Potion of Resistance        | 300        |
| 9   | Potion of Greater Healing   | 150        |
| 10  | Oil of Sharpness            | 3200       |

### Services

The shop is able to make most any potion but those not listed above require more money and time. The adventurers might even need to undertake a quest to find rare ingredients.

### Quest

#### A Dark Bargain

Morlyn needs a pouch of “special ingredients” from an herbalist in the woods. Seek out Butra and give her a name of anyone in town _except_ Morlyn. She’ll then provide you with the special ingredients. Return them to Morlyn and he’ll pay 2000 gp.
