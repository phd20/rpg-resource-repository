Shortly after the founding of Destiny’s Point, a  bandit named Vel Raza tried to take over the town. The **Sword of Destiny** defeated Vel Raza in single combat and built the first dungeon to hold her. Legend holds that Vel Raza never escaped the dungeons. But no records show her death and no cells are occupied by her to this day.

Nearly two centuries ago, a wall collapsed in the barracks of Razavel Dungeons. The guards built a staircase to explore where the opening led but little is known about what they found in the caverns beneath.

### Arrested

If your characters are arrested in Destiny’s Point, they will await trial in Razavel Dungeons. All belongings will remain guarded until the fine issued by the Council of Truth is paid in full.

Nowadays, long-term imprisonment is generally avoided as there aren’t enough dungeon cells to support it. But the Council of Truth will hand down harsh punishments for harsh crimes. These result in work in the mines or a total and permanent banishment from Destiny’s Point.
