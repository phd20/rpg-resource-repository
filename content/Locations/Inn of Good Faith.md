These warm wooden walls have heard it all. Most citizens are regulars at this warm and inviting inn. Lacadia runs a great kitchen and offers clean and safe rooms. In the evenings, Old Mott weaves wonderful tales by the hearth.

Travelers come from far and wide to see the Inn of Good Faith. Legend has it, it brings luck to those who dine or stay there.


> [!fantasy] Boon of Good Faith
> Any character that takes a [[08_RulesGlossary#Long Rest|Long Rest]] at the Inn of Good Faith gains Advantage on their next [[08_RulesGlossary#D20 Test|D20 Test]].

### Drink

1. Goodberry Mead. Regain a number of HP equal to your CON modifier.
2. Adventurer’s Rum. Gain Heroic Inspiration.
3. Fog tea. Gain Darkvision for 24 hours. If you already have Darkvision, your range doubles.
4. Kasnan coffee. During your next Short Rest, roll two dice for each Hit Point Die you spend to regain hit points.
