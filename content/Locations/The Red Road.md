A lone trade road runs the entirety of the forest (and some say the continent). The road is dangerous in places but not nearly as dangerous as wandering off of it. The road meets the River Innocence at Destiny’s Point.

The Red Road earned its name from the red color it takes on. Though no one can be sure the source, rumors say its simply red dust from a faraway desert along the trade road.
