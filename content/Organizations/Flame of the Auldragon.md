> _Flame of the Great Dragon God, live on in your disciples._

## Overview

Flame of the Auldragon is a monastic order of kobolds. One of the monasteries is in the Kobanu Pass north of Destiny’s Point.

## Goals

They worship the fallen god known as the Auldragon. 

## Methods & Resources

The group makes yearly treks to the lairs of powerful dragons that they see as the avatars of the Auldragon.

> [!secret]- GM Only—Hidden Agenda & Secrets
>
> They do not recognize the Dragonfather as a descendant of their god.
