 > The Order of Si-ehl is a monastic order that has a holding within the Storm Spire.

## Overview

A monastic order of martial artists seeking to understand balance and energy.

## Goals

They seek to understand balance and energy and channel them into martial arts. While their art can be deadly, it focuses much more on an expression of self through understanding than strictly combat.

## Methods & Resources

They follow a thousand year old text known as the _Way of Si-ehl_.

## Structure

Their structure is based on colored belts that represent one's journey thru Si-ehl.
