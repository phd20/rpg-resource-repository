 > The priests of the Storm Herald.

## Overview

The Storm Priests are comprised of priests, clerics, and even paladins that worship the Storm Herald. The Storm Priests were founded nearly 320 years ago when Sir Illyro Hallyford discovered the Cave of the Storms. Inside, ancient texts and artifacts showed the history of the Storm Herald and his followers.

## Goals

To worship the storm and the god behind it.

## Methods & Resources

They can appeal to their god for good weather as they know the rituals and rites that he requires. They charge a small fee of 2 gp to do so.
