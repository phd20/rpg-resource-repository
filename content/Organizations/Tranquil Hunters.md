> We are the tranquility of nature—the hunters in the wilderness.

## Overview

The Tranquil Hunters are a group of trackers and rangers that return to the Storm Spire to sell their furs, meat, and other goods. They hunt and range near Destiny’s Point and along the Red Road.

## Goals

The rangers that belong to the Tranquil Hunters have another, more secret purpose. They defend Destiny’s Point and the Red Road from from the ravages of monsters and humanoid hordes that press in from the wilderness.

> [!secret]- GM Only—Hidden Agenda & Secrets
>
> Rumors persist that _the Ghost_ is a Tranquil Hunter that never returned. They say you can sense her presence in a low fog as she watches and protects the more vulnerable parts of nature in the region.
